The recipe

Sound stays on this device. Phase 1 makes one short piece from a place, and the place itself is mixed in just as the microphone heard it, so nearby voices may be faintly audible. Spheres holds the place at a safe level so people in a recording cannot become clearly understandable. Only record where you are free to. Auditions, the gallery, export and voice come next.
