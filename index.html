The recipe

Sound stays on this device. The Sound Portrait mixes in the place as the microphone heard it, except for voices: anyone speaking nearby is sung through the music's own chords, so the voice itself is disguised. What is said can still be understood, such as a name or an address, so only record where you are free to. The gallery, export and sharing come next.
